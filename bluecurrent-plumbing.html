About the Project

BlueCurrent is a concept website for a residential and commercial plumbing business that needed to feel reliable, direct, and easy to act on.

The site direction focuses on strong service clarity, upfront trust cues, recent-work proof, review-led credibility, and a quote form that makes the next step feel simple for busy homeowners and businesses.

Service Clarity

Service cards and hero messaging quickly explain what the business handles and why someone should trust them.

Quote-Ready Flow

The contact section gathers the essentials clearly, helping users request help without feeling overwhelmed.

Trust Signals

Reviews, recent work, benefit cards, and straightforward copy make the concept feel grounded and dependable.
