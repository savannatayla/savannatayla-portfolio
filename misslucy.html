About the Project

Lucy Lingo is a bright ESL website for Lucinda, an online English teacher offering structured lessons for children and adults.

The updated live version includes clearer navigation, free resources, USD lesson packages, learner reviews, and a direct trial booking path so students and parents can move from interest to booking with less friction.

Clear Learning Paths

Kids and adult programs are split into simple cards so different audiences can quickly find the right lesson style.

Live Lesson Packages

The pricing section now presents USD lesson options with clear durations, use cases, and booking prompts.

Trust-Led Details

Friendly visuals, review cards, and confident calls to action help the site feel warm, credible, and ready for real learners.
